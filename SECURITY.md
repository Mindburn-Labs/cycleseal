# Security

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to
[security@mindburn.org](mailto:security@mindburn.org). Do not publish
vulnerability details in a public issue, pull request, or discussion.

cycleseal applies local, static policy to an agent cycle's command line and
signs a receipt chain that can be verified offline. Its policy does not govern
actions inside an allowed cycle. Reports can cover policy checks, receipt
signing, and offline verification.

In your private report, include:

- The affected release, tag, or commit and your operating environment.
- A minimal reproduction with the command, policy, and synthetic receipts
  needed to reproduce the behavior.
- The expected and actual behavior, likely security impact, and any known
  mitigation.

Do not include private signing keys, credentials, or confidential live receipt
chains. Use synthetic or redacted examples.
